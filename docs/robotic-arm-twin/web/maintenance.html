Conditions that can override a simple timeline

Oil-level checks are triggered by suspected leakage and relevant oil/repair work. Battery renewal can be triggered by a low-battery alert. Cable replacement depends on damage, cracks, and applicable lifetime limits. Refitting the balancing-device shaft triggers front-eye bearing lubrication. Cleaning frequency requires the actual working conditions. This starter covers the schedule table, not every event-driven requirement or procedure; confirm the applicable options, revision, procedures, service counters, and site plan before operational use.

Calendar deadlines use recorded completion dates plus the stated month interval. Hour deadlines use the recorded last-service DTC plus the stated hour interval. The initial 150-hour item is one-time and uses total DTC from commissioning; its completion must be recorded. Forecast calendar dates for hourly tasks assume the entered DTC accrual per day and do not replace the DTC deadline. Dates are calculated as calendar dates without time-of-day conversion.
