# External asset policy

External CAD geometry, datasheets, catalog records, standards references, and
software dependencies must have recorded provenance before entering the project.

- Prefer official APIs, plugins, or authorized downloads; do not scrape sources
  whose terms prohibit it.
- Record source URL or authorized locator, version/revision, access date,
  license or redistribution terms, checksum when practical, and reviewer
  disposition in the relevant registry or ledger.
- Do not commit credentials, proprietary standards text, restricted vendor
  manuals, or assets without redistribution rights.
- Do not represent placeholder geometry as manufacturer-accurate or a catalog
  feasibility result as final engineering approval.
- Preserve the original source and approval state when converting an asset into
  a normalized project record.
